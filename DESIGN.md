## 6. Motion and signature
Desktop only >=1024px, fine pointer, no reduced-motion preference. On each page load/refresh, a CSS entrance sequence starts immediately without waiting for hydration: a 1.6s nonblocking top progress stroke, headline line lifts staggered 120ms (900ms duration), description/action fade-lift (800ms), photograph scale reveal (1.4s). No fake percentage or forced loading gate; content and links are available throughout, animation fill is backwards only and clears afterward. Image readiness is represented independently with an on-load state and readable failure fallback. Scroll Craft scene grammar: hero photo translates up to 120px in its crop; wide classroom scene scrub-zooms 1.16 to 1.04 with 100px vertical travel; journey headings shift 96px, route line draws, resources lift and closing typography rises. Text remains fully opaque during scroll. GSAP context cleanup on navigation and media changes. Mobile/touch/reduced motion use static natural flow, no load or scroll animation, pins, parallax or reveal offsets. The current logo never animates or changes. Transform/opacity only. Hover max200ms. Do not force smooth document scrolling.
